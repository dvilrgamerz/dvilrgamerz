# Privacy Notice — DVILR SPEED

## What the static app does

DVILR SPEED performs browser-based network measurements by making requests to its configured measurement endpoints. The application does not require an account for its local features.

## Local history

Test history is stored in the browser's local storage on the device. Clearing site data/browser storage can remove that history.

## Network information

The browser and measurement infrastructure may expose normal connection metadata to the network/endpoint being contacted. DVILR SPEED should not display or store more network information than is needed for the feature.

## No guarantee of zero data collection

Hosting providers, CDNs, browsers, operating systems, and measurement endpoints can process technical request information under their own policies. Review those providers' current privacy documentation before commercial deployment.

## Analytics and cookies

The current product is intended to avoid unnecessary analytics and advertising trackers. If analytics, advertising, accounts, cookies, or other tracking are added later, update this notice and the application's consent/disclosure flow before deployment where legally required.

## Commercial deployment

This document is a product notice, not legal advice. A commercial launch should receive a jurisdiction-specific privacy review from a qualified lawyer, including applicable consumer privacy, children's privacy, cookies/tracking, data retention, and international-transfer requirements.
